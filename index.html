Who we are

Neighbors protecting neighbors.

Brooktrails Fire Department is an all-risk department serving Brooktrails, Spring Creek, Sylvandale, and nearby communities. Volunteer firefighters work alongside full-time leadership to answer calls, provide basic life support and rescue, prevent fires, and support neighboring agencies through mutual aid.

A place on the team

Bring your willingness. Start a conversation.

Train together, build practical skills, and serve your neighbors. Ask the department about the volunteer role, training, and application process.

Email Questions About Volunteering OR Call 707-459-4441

Our services

One team. Many ways to help.

From urgent response to year-round prevention, our work is centered on protecting life, property, and the place we call home.

Fire response

We respond to structure, wildland, vehicle, and other fires across our community and surrounding mutual-aid area.

Emergency medical support

Our basic life support response brings trained care to patients while additional medical resources are on the way.

Rescue

When someone is trapped, injured, or in danger, our crews bring the training and equipment needed to help.

Fire prevention

Prevention, inspections, and community education help reduce risk before an emergency begins.

Burn permits and air quality

Check before you light.

Open burning in Mendocino County requires approval from both the fire-safety and air-quality authorities. Check both every time you plan to burn.

Check the live CAL FIRE status before every burn

Burn rules change with conditions. The official CAL FIRE status and the daily Mendocino AQMD determination always control.

Fire safety

Check CAL FIRE status and permits

If burning is suspended, do not burn. When burning is allowed, confirm whether a CAL FIRE permit is required before ignition.

Check CAL FIRE status ↗

Air quality

Confirm today is a permissive burn day

Call the Mendocino County Air Quality Management District burn line on the day you plan to burn.
